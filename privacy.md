# Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 17, 2026
VOIDWARE Prohibited ("we," "us," or "our") operates this website (the "Site"). Because we are an independent, single-person software company, we believe in keeping things simple and respecting your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains what limited information is collected when you visit our Site and how it is used.

## 1. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not actively collect personal information (such as your name, address, or phone number) unless you voluntarily provide it to us.
• Information You Provide Directly: If you contact us via email or a contact form, we will receive your email address, name, and any information you choose to include in your message.
• Automatically Collected Information (Server Logs): Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically collects basic technical data when your browser requests a page. This may include your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring website, and the date and time of your visit.
• Cookies and Local Storage: This Site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. We may use essential local storage or strictly necessary cookies solely for basic site functionality (such as saving your light/dark theme preference).

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the limited information we collect solely to:

• Understand general, aggregated traffic patterns to improve our software documentation and web pages.

## 3. Third-Party Services & Links

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
• Hosting & Infrastructure: Our Site is hosted using third-party infrastructure (e.g., GitHub Pages and Cloudflare ). These providers may process your IP address to deliver the website to your browser securely.
• External Links: Our Site may contain links to external platforms where our software is hosted or distributed (such as GitHub, Codeberg, or software marketplaces). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites.

## 4. Data Retention

We keep emails and direct correspondence only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry or maintain basic business records. Standard server logs are automatically rotated and deleted by our hosting provider according to their standard retention schedules.

## 5. Children’s Privacy

Our Site and software are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.
