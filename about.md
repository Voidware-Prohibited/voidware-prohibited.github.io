VOIDWARE Prohibited is a Software Development, Operations Strategy and Consultancy Agency based out of the California Bay Area.

### Organizational Ethos
- Utilize the latest Technologies and Methodologies to empower Individuals and small independent Studios with AAA Features or better.
- Promote and Advance Electronic Freedom the Free and Open Source Software Movement.
- Maintain Modular, Configurable, Data-oriented architectures.
- Prioritize Performance and Optimization by Design.
- Encourage and Facilitate the use of Versioning Systems, Test-Driven Development (TDD), Continuous Integration/Continuous Development (CI/CD).
- Implement and Promote [Localization Best Practices](https://igda.org/resources-archive/best-practices-for-game-localization/) and [Accessibility Guidelines](https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/).
- No Monetization or Crypto. Ever.
- Humanity First.
- Do Cool Stuff.
